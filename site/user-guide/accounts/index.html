    Organisations, roles and policies

    Signing in

    In production AgentCanvas signs people in through your identity provider (any OpenID Connect provider: Keycloak, Okta, Entra ID, Auth0, Google…). The administrator configures it once (see Deploy and operate); people then click through to their usual login page. Sessions renew silently. The top bar shows who you are and your role, with Sign out and Sign in with a different account.

    In local mode (make dev-fake / make dev-api without OIDC) there is no sign-in: you are the built-in admin dev@localhost.

    Organisations

    Everything (projects, workflows, runs, secrets, memory, deployments, policies, audit log) belongs to an organisation, and nobody can see another organisation's data. A person can belong to several organisations: invitations to someone who already belongs to another organisation wait for them to accept (Home shows a banner), accepting switches to the new organisation, and AgentCanvas opens the one used last.

    Roles

    Role Can
    viewer see workflows and runs
    reviewer also answer approvals and human-input requests in the Inbox
    builder also build, run, test and publish workflows
    admin also manage members, policies, release gates and read the audit log

    Buttons for actions your role can't take are hidden.

    Members

    Admins open Members, enter an email, pick a role and click Invite. The invitation takes effect at the person's first sign-in with that (verified) email. Admins can change roles or remove people. An organisation can never be left without an admin who has signed in.

    To bootstrap a new install, list the first admins' emails in AGENTCANVAS_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_EMAILS; they become admins of the default organisation on first sign-in.

    Audit log

    Audit log (admins) records every change: who did it, what it touched, when and from where. Approvals record a hash of exactly what was approved; publishing past a failed release gate records the reason given. Rows are kept for 400 days by default.

    Policies

    Policies are organisation rules the editor and publishing enforce. Each policy has:

    • a scope: each step, each resource, or the workflow;
    • a match (CEL): which steps or resources it applies to, e.g. resource.type == "model.chat";
    • a require (CEL): what must hold, e.g. resource.config.model in ["anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5"];
    • a severity: error or warning;
    • optional environments: limit the rule to publishing to, e.g., prod.

    Two presets get you started:

    Preset Rule
    Writes need approval every step's external writes are gated by a human: node.effects.all(e, e.effect != "external_write" \|\| e.gated)
    Approved models in prod in prod, chat models must come from an approved list

    Names you can use: node (type, config, effects, ungated, models, tools), resource, workflow, env.

    Admins can Switch off a policy without deleting it. Broken policies show in the editor's Problems dock (E095; W095 for environment-only rules), steps inside subgraphs included. Draft and debug runs still work, but publishing is refused until every applicable policy holds.
