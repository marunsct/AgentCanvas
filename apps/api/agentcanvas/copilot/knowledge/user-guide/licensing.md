AgentCanvas is **source-available** under the **Business Source License 1.1** (BSL). The
[LICENSE](https://github.com/marunsct/AgentCanvas/blob/main/LICENSE) file is the licence; this page explains it in plain
words. Where they differ, the LICENSE file governs.

## Who may use it for free

| You are | Develop, test, evaluate, learn | Run it in production |
|---|---|---|
| Anyone, including large companies | Free | See the rows below |
| An individual, working for yourself | Free | **Free** |
| A small or medium organisation: fewer than 250 people (employees and individual contractors, counted with its affiliates), under US$50 million revenue in its last fiscal year, and under US$50 million external funding raised | Free | **Free** |
| A non-profit, school, college or university, for its non-commercial work | Free | **Free** |
| A larger organisation, or work for one | Free | **Needs a commercial licence** |
| Anyone offering AgentCanvas itself to others as a hosted service | Free | **Needs a commercial licence** |

- **Developing, testing, evaluating, prototyping, demonstrating and learning are free for everyone,** however large.
- **Production use** is anything else. It includes:
  - running workflows for real users or on live data;
  - internal tools and pilots, whatever the environment is called.
- **Working for a client:** if you are a freelancer or a small company building or running workflows for a client's production use, the client needs to qualify or hold a licence.
- **Your own customers don't count:** customers of your own product don't need anything just because your product uses AgentCanvas.
- **Growing past the limits:** if you grow beyond them, you have 90 days to get a commercial licence or stop production use.

## Hosted services

You may publish your own workflows, agents and deployments to your users, your customers or the public. That's what
deployments, share links, the MCP, A2A and AG-UI endpoints and the chat widget are for.

What needs a commercial licence is offering **AgentCanvas itself** to others, alone or inside another product. That
means its editor, Copilot, runtime, deployment endpoints or administration, used by third parties to build or run
workflows of their own. Installing and supporting AgentCanvas on a customer's own infrastructure is fine when that
customer qualifies or holds a licence.

## Your content and exported code

**Your content is yours.** That covers:

- your workflows, prompts and data;
- code you write yourself;
- the parts of an exported project that only express those, such as your prompts, names and settings.

The licence claims no rights in it.

**What an export carries:** an exported project also contains AgentCanvas's own runtime modules and code templates.
Those stay under the BSL, so every export includes `LICENSE-AgentCanvas.txt`. Running an export in production counts
as production use of AgentCanvas, with the same free and paid cases as above.

## It becomes open source

Each version of AgentCanvas becomes available under the **Apache License 2.0** four years after that version is first
published. From then on, anyone may use that version for any purpose under Apache 2.0.

## Commercial licences

For a commercial licence, or any other arrangement, open an issue titled "Commercial license" on
[GitHub](https://github.com/marunsct/AgentCanvas/issues), or contact the maintainer through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/marunsct).

Third-party components keep their own licences. They aren't part of the licensed work. The web app lists the licences
of the packages it bundles at `/third-party-licenses.txt`, and each image ships a CycloneDX bill of materials.
